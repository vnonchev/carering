Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

CareRing ("we", "us", "our") is a family caregiver coordination app operated by Vasil Nonchev, based in Sofia, Bulgaria. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal data when you use the CareRing mobile application and related services.

We take your privacy seriously, especially given the sensitive nature of health-related information. We comply with the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and applicable Bulgarian data protection laws.

You can withdraw that consent at any time in the app settings. The device advertising identifier is not collected — advertising-ID collection is turned off in the app, so no advertising ID is used even when analytics is enabled. No health or care data is ever sent to analytics.

2.5 Problem Reports You Send Us

If you use Settings → Report a problem, the app prepares an email to support@carering.app and shows you its complete contents first. Nothing is transmitted until you send it yourself from your own email app, and you can edit or delete any part of it before you do.

The prepared email contains a short report reference, your app version and build, your device model and Android version, your app language, your account identifier, your care circle identifier, and the time of your last successful sync. It deliberately contains no medication names, no names of people, no notes and no tasks, and we do not capture a screenshot or a recording of your screen. Anything else in the message is what you chose to write. We keep problem reports only as long as needed to resolve the issue and any follow-up with you.

2.6 People You Invite to a Household

When you invite someone to your household by email, we process that person's email address so the invite can reach them, even if they do not use CareRing yet:

  • We send them one invite email through our email provider (Resend, see Section 5.2). The email is generic: it contains no household name, no name of yours and no care data.
  • On the invite we store only a one-way code (a keyed hash) of the address, used to match the invite when they sign in, and may store a partly hidden, encrypted form of it (for example a***b@gmail.com) so you can see whom you invited. The full address is never stored on the invite.
  • To deliver the email, the full address is kept, encrypted, in our sending queue for up to 2 days after delivery (up to 30 days if delivery fails).
  • An unanswered invite expires after 7 days. Invite records are deleted when the household is deleted, or when you or the invited person delete a CareRing account.

We use the address only to deliver and match this invite. The invited person can ask us to delete the invite at any time (Section 13).

3. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR Article 6)

  • Consent — You voluntarily provide health and care data by entering it into the app. Optional analytics (Section 2.4) runs only with your explicit opt-in consent.
  • Contract performance — Processing is necessary to provide the CareRing service you signed up for.
  • Legitimate interest — Crash reporting and technical diagnostics to maintain service quality; delivering a household invite to the email address you enter (Section 2.6).

Health data is processed under GDPR Article 9(2)(a) — explicit consent. You may withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account.

4. How We Use Your Data

  • Display care information to members of your care circles
  • Send push notifications and emails for medication reminders, task assignments, care updates, and optional daily digests, and the household invite emails you ask us to send
  • Synchronize data between your devices and care circle members
  • Generate care reports (PDF export) when you request them
  • Monitor app crashes and fix bugs

We do NOT:

  • Sell your data to anyone
  • Use your data for advertising
  • Share your data with data brokers
  • Use your health data for profiling or automated decision-making
  • Train AI or machine learning models on your data

6. Data Storage & Security

  • Application servers: Google Cloud Platform, region europe-west1 (Belgium, EU). Data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.2+). Sensitive personal fields are additionally encrypted at the application layer (AES-256-GCM) before storage, and data at rest is encrypted by our cloud database and storage providers.
  • Database: Hosted by Supabase, region eu-central-2 (Zurich, Switzerland — covered by a European Commission adequacy decision; see Section 11). Data encrypted at rest by Supabase and in transit (TLS 1.2+).
  • Device: Local database encrypted with SQLCipher (AES-256). Sensitive preferences are encrypted using the Android Keystore where available.
  • Authentication: Firebase Authentication with Google Sign-In. We never see or store your Google password.
  • Access control: Role-based access within care circles (Owner, Admin, Member, Viewer).

7. Data Retention

  • Your data is retained for as long as your account exists.
  • When you delete your account, the data described in Section 9 is permanently deleted within 30 days.
  • Crash reports are retained for 90 days.
  • Server logs are retained for 30 days.
  • Household invites: see Section 2.6.

8. Your Rights (GDPR Articles 15-22)

You have the right to:

  • Access — Request a copy of all data we hold about you
  • Rectification — Correct inaccurate data (you can edit directly in the app)
  • Erasure — Delete your account and associated data ("right to be forgotten"), subject to the limited retention described in Section 9
  • Data portability — Export your data in a machine-readable format (JSON)
  • Restriction — Request that we limit processing of your data
  • Objection — Object to processing based on legitimate interest
  • Withdraw consent — Withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account

To exercise any of these rights, use the in-app settings or contact us at privacy@carering.app. We will respond within 30 days.

9. Account Deletion

You can delete your account at any time from Settings within the app. This will:

  • Permanently delete the care circles you own and all data within them
  • Permanently delete your user profile and account data
  • Remove you from care circles owned by other members. Your past entries in those shared circles (for example, activity and inventory history) are kept for the remaining members' records, but your name is removed from them
  • Delete your authentication identity held by our authentication provider (Firebase Authentication)
  • Request deletion of your subscription record held by our subscription processor (RevenueCat)

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Deletion of the records held by Firebase and RevenueCat is carried out on a best-effort basis immediately after your account data is removed. Some non-identifying entries in shared care circles may be retained — without your name — so that the other members' care history stays intact.

10. Children's Privacy

You must be at least 18 years old to create a CareRing account. Care recipients whose information is entered in the app may be of any age, including minors — their information is entered and managed by a responsible adult caregiver, who must have the authority to provide it. CareRing is not intended to be used directly by anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly allow a person under 18 to create their own account. If you believe someone under 18 has created an account, please contact us and we will delete it.

11. International Data Transfers

Our application servers run in the EU on Google Cloud Platform (europe-west1, Belgium). Our database is hosted by Supabase in Switzerland (eu-central-2, Zurich). Switzerland is not part of the EU/EEA, but the European Commission has granted it an adequacy decision, so storing your data there is a lawful transfer under GDPR Article 45 and your data receives protection equivalent to EU standards. Beyond this, some limited data — push notifications, emails (including daily digests that may name a care recipient), crash reports, opt-in analytics, and subscription status — is handled by Google (Firebase), Resend (email) and RevenueCat and may be processed outside the EU/Switzerland, including in the United States, under appropriate safeguards (Standard Contractual Clauses and/or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework). Your core care records in the database remain in Switzerland.
