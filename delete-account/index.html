Delete Your Account

How to Delete Your Account

You can permanently delete your CareRing account and all associated data directly from the app.

  1. Open the CareRing app on your Android device
  2. Go to Settings (tap the gear icon in the bottom navigation)
  3. Scroll down and tap "Delete My Account"
  4. Confirm the deletion when prompted
This action is permanent and cannot be undone. Once your account is deleted, all of your data will be permanently removed from our servers.

What Gets Deleted

When you delete your account, the following data is permanently removed:

  1. Your user profile (name, email, photo)
  2. All care circles you own (and all data within them)
  3. Your membership in care circles owned by others
  4. All medications, schedules, and medication logs you created
  5. All tasks, appointments, and care notes you created
  6. All daily check-ins you submitted
  7. Medical conditions, allergies, and emergency contacts in your circles
  8. Activity history and notification preferences

Can't Access the App?

If you have uninstalled CareRing or are unable to access the app, you can request account deletion by emailing us at privacy@carering.app. Please include the email address associated with your Google Sign-In so we can locate your account. We will process your request within 30 days.
